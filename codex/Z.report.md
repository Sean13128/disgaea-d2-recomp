**Fixed in the SDK.** W's existing 50 ms reset already discarded long backlogs, but permitted up to nine short overdue periods to burst. A 30 ms mixer stall reproduced persistent alternating silence even after the clock caught up. Device room also cannot safely be filled by consuming extra guest blocks.

Changes:
- `ps3recomp/libs/audio/cellAudio.c`: discard deadline debt above three blocks (~16 ms); reserve at least 4.33 ms after each notification (1 ms scheduling jitter budget), so smaller delays cannot cause immediate catch-up consumption. Keep the nominal 187.5 Hz clock and device backpressure. Refill an empty SDL/WASAPI device with silence without advancing guest indices or issuing extra events. SDL primes two callback periods, bounded to 2–4 blocks. macOS mixer uses interactive QoS. `AUDIO_RATE` reports recovery block, lag, missed periods and silence priming.
